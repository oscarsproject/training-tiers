Tiers are not mutually exclusive: a single project may appear in more than one tier. 10 projects are in all three tiers.

Tier 1: the project organised a training event (workshop, webinar, school, hackathon, course) or taught a session at another organisation's training event. Co-design meetings, focus groups, feedback workshops and talks at other events are not counted. Items marked with a note reflect decisions recorded on 5 October 2026.

Tier 2: learning materials produced by the project (tutorials, guides, textbooks, recorded lectures, teaching notebooks), including end-user guides and manuals, which remain listed as Technical Documentation in the results portfolio. A webinar's recording counts here while the webinar itself counts in Tier 1. Slides of project presentations are not counted.

Tier 3: the project reports new skills or capacities built within its team or community (final survey).

This facilitates more informed and wildlife-friendly planning and mitigation strategies, ultimately supporting better integration of biodiversity considerations into transport infrastructure development.
